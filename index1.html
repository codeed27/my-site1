JUPITER

Jupiter is the largest planet in our Solar System and the fifth planet from the Sun. It is a gas giant, mainly made up of hydrogen and helium. Jupiter is much larger than Earth and has a very strong gravitational pull.

One of Jupiter’s most famous features is the Great Red Spot, a huge storm that has existed for centuries. Jupiter also has a faint ring system and many moons. Its four largest moons are Io, Europa, Ganymede, and Callisto.

Jupiter rotates very quickly, completing one rotation in about 10 hours. However, because it is so far from the Sun, it takes about 12 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun.

Jupiter is an important planet for scientists because studying it helps us understand the formation and development of our Solar System. Its huge size, powerful storms, magnetic field, and fascinating
moons make it one of the most interesting planets to study.
